Stanford GSB Professor Jung Ho Choi and Utah Professor Sara Malik approached my team, Data, Analytics, and Research Computing (DARC), to work with Glassdoor to acquire five data pillars for this research paper. It became abundantly clear that Glassdoor wasn't willing to work with academia, so I built several webscrapes that collected employee reviews and salary compensation data at scale.
