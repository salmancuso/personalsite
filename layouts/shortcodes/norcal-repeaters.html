Read these with care

  • Emergency power is what owners report to RepeaterBook. Mt Oso (WD6EJF) batteries ran down in a 2-day PG&E shutoff in 2025.
  • Elevation is measured only where the location is exact, or borrowed from another repeater at the same named site within 40 km. City-center locations are left blank.
  • Club research came from web search results and RadioReference listings. Low-confidence rows deserve a check before you rely on them.
  • Only 2m and 70cm machines that are not off air and not private are ranked.
